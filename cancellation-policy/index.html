Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Automatic renewal

FlatRx monthly plans automatically renew and are billed every month. Quarterly plans automatically renew and are billed every 3 months. Both continue until you cancel. Your payment method on file is charged the plan price shown at checkout at each renewal; on a quarterly plan, the full 3-month price is charged at checkout and at each renewal. If you pay for a 3-month plan with a buy-now-pay-later option, renewals can't be charged to it automatically: at each renewal you'll be asked to complete the payment in the FlatRx patient portal, and your next 3-month supply is prepared only after you pay. If your plan price changes, we will email you at least 30 days before the change applies, with the new price, the date it applies and how to cancel. You may cancel before it takes effect.

How to cancel

You may cancel at any time. Message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io. A cancellation request we receive before your next renewal is charged stops that renewal. A renewal charged before you cancel is not refunded. If a renewal is charged after we received your cancellation request, we refund that charge in full.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. A monthly plan ends at the close of the current month of your plan; a quarterly plan ends at the close of the current 3-month period. Cancellation stops future renewals. If you cancel after a renewal has been charged, that charge is not refunded: that period's medication ships, and your plan ends at the close of that period.

How to pause

To take a break, message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io. Your plan remains active through the end of the current billing period. A team member will arrange a pause and a resume date you choose. While paused, your plan does not renew and you are not billed. Request the pause before your next charge; a renewal charged before you ask to pause is not refunded, and one charged after we received your request is refunded. Billing at your plan price and shipments restart on your selected resume date. Before then, message your provider: your provider decides whether restarting treatment, and at what dose, is appropriate after a break.

Refunds

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products.

Cancelling or pausing does not make a billing period that has already been charged refundable. Your first shipment is fully refundable until the pharmacy prepares it. After that, except for the refunds listed in this policy, each billing period is non-refundable once it has been charged. Quarterly plans ship as a single 3-month supply.

You receive a full refund if you cancel before your first shipment is prepared, if your licensed provider determines after review that you are not medically eligible for treatment, or if a renewal is charged after we received your cancellation or pause request. See our Refund Policy for all refund terms, damaged or missing delivery reports, billing errors, and request timelines.
