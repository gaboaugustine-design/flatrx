Subscriptions, billing, and automatic renewal

  • What renews and when: Your FlatRx plan automatically renews at the end of each billing period until you cancel. Monthly plans renew and are billed every month. Quarterly plans renew and are billed every 3 months. Each plan you hold is billed, renewed, and cancelled separately.
  • Recurring charges: At each renewal, your payment method on file is charged the plan price shown at checkout (on a quarterly plan, the full 3-month price), unless the price has changed as described below or you cancelled first.
  • Price changes: If we raise your plan's price or materially change its renewal terms, we will email you at least 30 days before the change applies to your plan, with the new price, the date it takes effect and how to cancel. If you don't want the change, cancel before that date and you will not be charged the new price.
  • Payment method: You authorize FlatRx to charge your payment method for your plan price. The plan price shown before checkout is the total FlatRx charges each billing period: it includes your provider's review, prescribed medication and shipping, with no other FlatRx fees. If you choose a pay-later option, that provider's own terms, including any interest or fees, apply separately. Your first charge is made at checkout, before your provider's review; renewals are charged every month or every 3 months, depending on your plan. Keep your payment information current. If payment fails, we may suspend or cancel your subscription.
  • Pay-later options: Where a buy-now-pay-later option is offered at checkout (3-month plans), renewals can't be charged to it automatically. You'll be asked to complete each renewal payment in the FlatRx patient portal, and your next shipment is prepared after that payment.
  • How to cancel: Message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io. A cancellation request we receive before your renewal is charged stops that renewal, even if we process it afterward; if a renewal is charged after we received your request, we refund that charge in full. A renewal charged before you cancel is not refunded.
  • When cancellation takes effect: Your plan ends at the close of the current billing period: the current month of a monthly plan or the current 3-month period of a quarterly plan. Cancellation stops future renewals. If you cancel after a renewal has been charged, that charge is not refunded: that period's medication ships, and your plan ends at the close of that period.
  • Pausing: You may request a pause through the same channels. Your plan remains active through the current billing period. While paused, you are not billed and the plan does not renew; billing at your plan price and shipments restart on the resume date you arrange with the care team; your provider decides whether restarting treatment, and at what dose, is appropriate after a break. See our Cancellation Policy.

Your first shipment is fully refundable until the pharmacy prepares it. After that, a billing period is not refundable once charged, except as stated in these Terms and our Refund Policy. Quarterly plans ship as a single 3-month supply.

You receive a full refund if you cancel before your first shipment is prepared or if your licensed provider determines after review that you are not medically eligible for treatment. See our Refund Policy for all refund terms, damaged and missing deliveries, billing errors, and refund timelines.
