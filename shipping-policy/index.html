Shipping Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Where we ship

Medication ships to all 50 states. We do not ship outside the United States.

Who prepares and ships medication

Independent licensed providers prescribe compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide only when appropriate after an online visit. FlatRx does not prescribe, compound, or dispense medication. US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies prepare and ship medication; the pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products.

Plan shipments

Monthly plans ship one month at a time. Quarterly plans ship as a single 3-month supply. On a 3-month plan paid with a buy-now-pay-later option, your next supply ships after you complete the renewal payment in your patient portal. If your medication can't ship within 30 days of a charge, we'll tell you and you may cancel that order for a full refund.

Tracking information is provided when available.

Shipping charges

There is no shipping charge.

Your shipping address

You are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address. Refunds are not available when you provide an incorrect shipping address, subject to the exceptions in our Refund Policy. For shipping questions, message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io.

Damaged or missing deliveries

Report damage within 24 hours of delivery with photographs. After verification, the product will be replaced or a refund issued.

If the carrier confirms delivery but you did not receive your package, report it within 24 hours of the carrier's delivery confirmation, with supporting documentation. A police report may be required for suspected theft. If a package is lost in transit before delivery is confirmed, contact us and we will work with the pharmacy and the carrier to resolve it. FlatRx is not responsible for packages after the carrier confirms delivery, except as stated in our Refund Policy.

Send reports through the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or to support at support@flatrx.io. See our Refund Policy for refund eligibility and request timelines.
