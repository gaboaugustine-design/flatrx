Refund Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Plans covered

This policy applies to FlatRx plans that include compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide, available only if a licensed provider finds treatment appropriate after an online visit. Plans are billed monthly or every 3 months. Each plan is billed, renewed, and cancelled separately.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products.

Automatic renewal and cancellation

Your monthly plan automatically renews every month, and your quarterly plan automatically renews every 3 months, until you cancel. Your payment method on file is charged the plan price shown at checkout at each renewal; on a quarterly plan, the full 3-month price is charged at checkout and at each renewal. If you pay for a 3-month plan with a buy-now-pay-later option, renewals can't be charged to it automatically: at each renewal you'll be asked to complete the payment in the FlatRx patient portal, and your next 3-month supply is prepared only after you pay. If your plan price changes, we will email you at least 30 days before the change applies, with the new price, the date it applies and how to cancel. You may cancel before it takes effect.

You may cancel or pause at any time by messaging the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or emailing support at support@flatrx.io. A cancellation request we receive before your next renewal is charged stops that renewal. A renewal charged before you cancel is not refunded. If a renewal is charged after we received your cancellation request, we refund that charge in full.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period: the end of the current month of a monthly plan or the end of the current 3-month period of a quarterly plan. Cancellation stops future renewals. The full-refund exceptions below still apply. If you cancel after a renewal has been charged, that charge is not refunded: that period's medication ships, and your plan ends at the close of that period.

Pausing

To pause instead of cancel, message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io. Your plan remains active through the end of the current billing period. A team member will arrange the pause and a resume date you choose. While paused, your plan does not renew and you are not billed. Request the pause before your next charge; a renewal charged before you ask to pause is not refunded, and one charged after we received your request is refunded. Billing at your plan price and shipments restart on your selected resume date. Before then, message your provider: your provider decides whether restarting treatment, and at what dose, is appropriate after a break. See our Cancellation Policy.

Preparation, shipping, and non-refundable medication

FlatRx does not prescribe, compound, or dispense medication. Independent licensed providers of Wasef Health, PC prescribe. US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies prepare and ship medication; the pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label.

Monthly plans ship one month at a time. Your first shipment is fully refundable until the pharmacy prepares it. After that, except for the refunds listed in this policy, each billing period is non-refundable once it has been charged. Quarterly plans ship as a single 3-month supply.

Medication prepared and labelled specifically for you cannot be returned for a refund. This includes unopened vials and unused medication. Individual results vary; dissatisfaction with results, a change of mind, or deciding not to continue does not qualify an order for a refund. The full-refund, damaged-delivery, and billing-error exceptions below still apply.

Refunds are not available for an incorrect shipping address you provide, a material violation of our Terms of Service (for example, fraud), or a package after the carrier confirms delivery, subject to the full refunds, verified damaged deliveries, and billing-error refunds below. Report a missing package as described below so the circumstances can be reviewed.

Full refunds

You receive a full refund if you cancel before your first shipment is prepared, if your licensed provider determines after review that you are not medically eligible for treatment, or if a renewal is charged after we received your cancellation or pause request. If your medication can't ship within 30 days of a charge, we'll tell you and you may cancel that order for a full refund.

How refunds are returned

When a refund is due, you can take it as cash back to your original payment method, or as FlatRx account credit worth 15% more, applied toward future care. Cash is the default unless you choose credit. Account credit has no cash value and can't be transferred. A refund on a pay-later purchase is returned to the pay-later provider.

Damaged deliveries

Report damage within 24 hours of delivery and provide photographs. After verification, the product will be replaced or a refund issued. Message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io.

Missing packages

If the carrier confirms delivery but you did not receive your package, report it within 24 hours of the carrier's delivery confirmation, with supporting documentation, by messaging the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or emailing support at support@flatrx.io. A police report may be required for suspected theft. If a package is lost in transit before delivery is confirmed, contact us and we will work with the pharmacy and the carrier to resolve it. FlatRx is not responsible for packages after the carrier confirms delivery, except as stated in this policy.

Requesting a refund

Message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io, with your order details and supporting documentation. Refund requests are reviewed within 2–4 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Billing errors

Duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors receive refunds.
