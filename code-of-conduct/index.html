Physician Code of Conduct

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Introduction

These standards describe what FlatRx expects of the independent licensed providers of Wasef Health, PC who care for patients through the FlatRx platform. They guide patient communication, clinical documentation, safety, privacy, and professional conduct. FlatRx does not prescribe, compound, or dispense medication; licensed providers make clinical decisions using independent professional judgment.

Clinical questions, coverage and licensure notices go to Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership. FlatRx handles platform and technical issues only. These standards may evolve with provider feedback.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products.

Best practices on the platform

  • Be patient: Telehealth may be unfamiliar to patients. Answer questions with sensitivity and guide patients through their care.
  • Explain decisions: Explain your clinical decisions so patients understand your guidance, including when you decide not to prescribe medication.
  • Follow up: Use the platform's messaging system to arrange follow-up when closer monitoring is needed. Contact Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership for assistance.
  • Document: Maintain detailed records of clinical decisions, including additional information obtained directly from patients. Document your assessment of contraindications and risks, including any raised by system alerts.
  • Check messages: Check patient messages daily. Notify Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership in advance if you expect to be unavailable for more than 48 hours so patient support can be arranged.

Conduct expectations

  • Verify identity: Confirm patient identity and ensure information supplied matches the identity documentation reviewed.
  • Follow state protocols: Follow state-specific requirements for establishing a patient-provider relationship before offering medical advice or treatment. When uncertain, consult Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership.
  • Respond promptly: Address consultations in a timely manner.
  • One patient at a time: Give each consultation your full attention. Do not intervene in another provider's ongoing treatment unless a second opinion is requested.

Safety, security, and privacy

  • Review drug interactions: Review all reported medications for interactions. Seek clinical guidance for complex cases through Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership.
  • Use caution: If you are uncertain about treatment safety, seek consultation or refer the patient to their primary healthcare provider rather than prescribe.
  • Address underlying concerns: Consider physical and mental health concerns and follow appropriate protocols for emergencies or concerning behavior.
  • Review protocols: Consult the provider group's condition-specific protocols and make medical decisions according to the standard of care.
  • Protect credentials: Keep provider account credentials confidential. Do not disclose patient information outside the uses and disclosures permitted for care and platform operations.
  • Secure protected health information: Share patient information through HIPAA-compliant channels. Avoid using the platform in public settings where protected health information could be exposed.

Ethical expectations

  • Do no harm: Prioritize patient safety. Compensation must not influence treatment decisions.
  • No self-referral: Refer patients needing additional services to their primary healthcare provider or an external physician rather than to your private practice.
  • Practice unimpaired: Do not practice on the platform while under the influence of substances that could impair judgment.
  • Report professional changes: Promptly inform Wasef Health, PC clinical leadership of professional disciplinary action or changes to your medical licensure status.

Commitment to care

These standards support careful clinical judgment, respectful communication, and protection of patient information. Patients with questions about these standards can message the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or email support at support@flatrx.io.
