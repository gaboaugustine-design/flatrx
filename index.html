We've got you.

You have questions, we have answers.

How does FlatRx work?

You answer health questions online and pay for your plan at checkout. A licensed, independent provider then reviews your answers and decides whether treatment is appropriate for you. If a prescription is written, a US-licensed compounding pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to your door. You can message your provider through your portal.

Is this FDA-approved?

No. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. They are prepared by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies under a prescription from a licensed provider. Read the Important Safety Information.

Will I be prescribed?

Only if a licensed, independent provider reviews your information and decides treatment is appropriate for you. FlatRx does not prescribe medication or give medical advice.

What is the difference between compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide?

Both are taken as a weekly injection. Compounded semaglutide contains semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist; compounded tirzepatide contains tirzepatide, which also acts on a second receptor, GIP. Your provider decides which medication, if either, is appropriate for you.

How do the plans work?

Each medication has two plans. Compounded semaglutide: $99 billed every month, or $267 billed every 3 months ($89/mo). Compounded tirzepatide: $119 billed every month, or $327 billed every 3 months ($109/mo). Every plan renews automatically until you cancel; if a price ever changes, we email you at least 30 days before. Both include the provider review, your medication if prescribed, shipping, and provider messaging. The price does not change when your provider adjusts your dose.

Are there any other fees?

No. There is no membership fee, no consultation fee, and no insurance requirement. The plan price is the whole price. If you choose a pay-later option on a 3-month plan, that provider's own terms, including any interest or fees, apply.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans renew automatically until you cancel. To cancel, message your care team in the patient portal or email support@flatrx.io before your next renewal is charged. See our Cancellation Policy and Refund Policy.

Where does the medication come from?

From US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. Which pharmacy fills your prescription depends on your state and the formulation your provider prescribes. The pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label.

How is it shipped?

Directly to your door. Tracking is provided when available. Refrigerate your medication when it arrives. See our Shipping Policy.

Which states are eligible?

FlatRx is available in every US state except California. Your state is confirmed during signup, before you pay.

How do I contact support?

Email support@flatrx.io or call (631) 488-0789. If you have an account, you can also message the care team in your patient portal. See our contact page.
