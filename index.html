You have questions, we have answers.

How does Brand Name work?

You answer health questions online. A licensed, independent provider reviews them and decides whether treatment is appropriate for you. If a prescription is written, a US-licensed compounding pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to your door. You can message your provider through your portal.

Is this FDA-approved?

No. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and have not been evaluated by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality. They are prepared by state-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies under a prescription from a licensed provider.

Will I be prescribed?

Only if a licensed, independent provider reviews your information and decides treatment is appropriate for you. Brand Name does not prescribe medication or give medical advice.

What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Both are GLP-1 receptor agonists taken as a weekly injection. Tirzepatide also acts on a second receptor, GIP. Your provider decides which medication, if either, is appropriate for you.

How do the plans work?

Each medication has an annual plan, billed once a year at the lower monthly rate, and a month-to-month plan. Both include the provider review, your medication if prescribed, shipping, and provider messaging. The price does not change when your provider adjusts your dose.

Are there any other fees?

No. There is no membership fee, no consultation fee, and no insurance requirement. The plan price is the whole price.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, by contacting support. See the refund policy for unshipped medication.

Where does the medication come from?

From US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies. Which pharmacy fills your prescription depends on your state and the formulation your provider prescribes.

How is it shipped?

Directly to your door with tracking. You'll receive a notification when it ships. Refrigerate your medication when it arrives.

Which states are eligible?

Availability depends on your state and is confirmed during signup before you pay.
