Who we are

FlatRx is the storefront operated by Leanio Health LLC, doing business as FlatRx. It facilitates access to independent licensed healthcare providers and pharmacy services through flatrx.io and the FlatRx patient portal, hosted by our technology platform provider.

FlatRx does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, prescribe, compound, or dispense medication. Independent licensed providers make clinical decisions. Provider group: Wasef Health, PC. US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies prepare and ship medication; the pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products.

This policy addresses personal information associated with the website, patient portal, and administrative services used to facilitate access to care, and includes the Notice of Privacy Practices for protected health information.

HIPAA privacy statement

Notice of Privacy Practices for Protected Health Information

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

This Notice is provided by Wasef Health, PC, the independent medical group whose licensed providers care for FlatRx patients, and covers the protected health information it creates and receives. Leanio Health LLC, doing business as FlatRx, and our technology platform provider handle that information on the medical group's behalf under business associate agreements. The pharmacy that dispenses your medication is a separate healthcare provider and gives you its own notice.

This Notice of Privacy Practices (“Notice”) describes how the responsible entity identified above may use and disclose protected health information (“PHI”) for treatment, payment, healthcare operations, and other purposes permitted or required by law. It also describes patient rights. In this Notice, “we” refers to that responsible entity. Identifying the responsible entity does not make FlatRx a medical provider or pharmacy.

We are required by law to maintain the privacy of PHI, provide this Notice of legal duties and privacy practices, and abide by its terms.

Other uses and disclosures of PHI not described in this Notice, including most uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes, uses and disclosures for marketing, and sales of PHI, will be made only with your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent action has already been taken in reliance on it.

Uses and disclosures of PHI

  • Treatment: To provide, coordinate, or manage healthcare and related services, including communications among providers about treatment and care coordination.
  • Payment: To obtain payment for healthcare services, including billing and collection. FlatRx plans are paid directly by you; health insurance is not billed.
  • Healthcare operations: For quality assessment and improvement, case management, accreditation, licensing, credentialing, medical reviews, audits, and legal services.
  • As required by law: When federal, state, or local law requires disclosure.
  • Public health and safety: To prevent or control disease, injury, or disability; report child abuse or neglect, medication reactions, or product problems; or notify people of communicable disease exposure or risks.
  • Health oversight: For authorized audits, investigations, inspections, and licensure activities.
  • Judicial and administrative proceedings: In response to a court or administrative order, subpoena, discovery request, or other lawful process.
  • Law enforcement: For purposes such as reporting certain wounds or injuries or complying with a court order, warrant, or other legal process.
  • Research: When research has institutional review board approval and privacy protections are in place.
  • Organ and tissue donation: To organizations handling organ procurement, transplantation, or donation if you are an organ donor.
  • Workers' compensation: For programs providing benefits for work-related injuries or illnesses.
  • Military and veterans: As required by military authorities if you are a member of the armed forces.
  • Inmates: To the correctional institution or law enforcement official having custody if you are an inmate.

Your rights regarding PHI

Submit written requests to the Privacy Officer by email at support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”) or by calling (631) 488-0789.

  • Inspect and copy: You may inspect and copy PHI maintained about you, with certain exceptions. A reasonable fee may apply for copying, mailing, or associated supplies.
  • Amend: You may request an amendment if information is incorrect or incomplete. Identify the information and explain why it should change. A request may be denied if the information is accurate and complete or was not created by the entity receiving the request.
  • Accounting of disclosures: You may request an accounting of disclosures in the past six years, excluding treatment, payment, healthcare operations, and certain other disclosures.
  • Restrictions: You may request restrictions on uses or disclosures for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations. Specify the restriction and to whom it applies. We will consider the request but are not required to agree, except that we must agree to a request not to disclose PHI to your health plan for an item or service you paid for in full out of pocket.
  • Confidential communications: You may request communications about PHI in a particular way or at a particular location. Specify how or where you wish to be contacted.
  • Paper copy: You may receive a paper copy of this Notice even if you agreed to receive it electronically.
  • Breach notification: You have the right to notification if a breach of your PHI is discovered.

Transmission of PHI

We use encryption in transit and other safeguards required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”) Security Rule to protect electronic PHI.

Changes to this Notice

We may change this Notice. The revised Notice applies to PHI already held and information received in the future. The current Notice will be made available on the website and will contain its effective date. To get a paper copy, email support@flatrx.io or call (631) 488-0789.

Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may complain to the Privacy Officer or the Secretary of the Department of Health and Human Services. You will not face retaliation for filing a complaint.

State-specific health information protections

States may provide additional protections and rights regarding PHI.

California residents

  • Access: You may request PHI in a readily usable electronic format and additional information required by California law by writing to the Privacy Officer.
  • Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (“CMIA”): California provides additional medical information protections under CMIA. Those protections apply in addition to HIPAA where required.
  • Marketing and sale of PHI: We will not use or disclose PHI for marketing, or sell PHI, without your written authorization.
  • Minors' rights: California provides rights concerning disclosure to a parent or guardian of information about certain sensitive services, such as reproductive health, mental health, or substance use disorder treatment. These privacy protections do not change FlatRx's age-18 eligibility requirement.

Written consent and additional protections for medical information will be applied as required by California law. Send requests to support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”).

New York residents

New York provides additional privacy protections for HIV-related information, mental health records, and genetic testing results. Where New York law requires written consent before HIV-related, mental health or genetic testing information is disclosed, the responsible entity obtains that consent.

Texas residents

The Texas Medical Privacy Act provides protections beyond HIPAA, including consent for certain disclosures, safeguards for electronic PHI, and destruction requirements. Texas also provides protections for mental health and substance use treatment records.

Florida residents

Florida provides additional protections for mental health records, HIV/AIDS-related information, and substance abuse treatment records. Written consent before disclosure and electronic PHI security measures will be applied as required by Florida law.

Illinois residents

Illinois provides additional protections for mental health records, HIV/AIDS-related information, and genetic testing results. Written consent before disclosure and notification of unauthorized access to electronic PHI will be provided as required by Illinois law.

Massachusetts residents

Massachusetts provides additional protections for mental health records, HIV/AIDS-related information, and genetic testing results. Written consent before disclosure and electronic PHI security measures will be applied as required by Massachusetts law.

Contact the Privacy Officer at support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”) for information about additional protections in your state.

Personal information associated with the website and Services

In the following sections, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to FlatRx. The PHI Notice above addresses protected health information; these sections address other personal information associated with the Services.

Information you provide

Depending on your use of the Services, information you provide may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and mailing address supplied during account creation or an assessment.
  • Payment and billing information associated with a purchase.
  • Communications, including support requests and feedback.

Uses of personal information

  • Facilitating care: Connecting patients with independent licensed providers, coordinating consultations, and facilitating prescription fulfillment by pharmacies.
  • Accounts: Creating and maintaining accounts, authenticating identity, and communicating about accounts and services.
  • Payments: Processing payments, administering subscriptions, and handling billing questions.
  • Service communications: Sending account, order, and provider-related notices.
  • Safety and security: Detecting fraud, protecting the platform, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • Signup source: Measuring which pages, links or campaigns led to sign-ups. We do not use it to target ads to you and do not share it with advertising platforms.
  • Legal compliance: Complying with applicable laws and legal processes and responding to lawful public authority requests.

Text messaging

FlatRx does not send marketing text messages. If we text you about your account, an order or your care, message and data rates may apply, and you can ask us to stop by emailing support@flatrx.io.

Cookies, tracking, analytics, and advertising data sharing

FlatRx has not installed advertising pixels or third-party analytics tools, and does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Our website host and technology platform automatically collect limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. When you create an account, the platform records the web-address codes (such as UTM parameters) and cookies on our storefront domain at signup and stores them with your account record. This information is not shared with advertising platforms. Our technology platform provider also uses its own product-analytics tools on the patient portal and online visit pages to operate, secure and improve its platform.

We will update this policy, with a new effective date, before enabling any advertising pixel, analytics tool or ad-platform integration, and will email account holders about material changes.

Other third-party disclosures

Information may be shared with service providers supporting operations, such as payment processing, hosting, and service communications, under obligations to protect it; with licensed providers and pharmacies to facilitate care as described in the PHI Notice; or as required by law, regulation, or legal process.

California privacy rights under CCPA/CPRA

California residents have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA”) and California Privacy Rights Act (“CPRA”), in addition to applicable PHI rights described above:

  • Know: Request categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, sources, purposes, and categories of recipients.
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: Opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising by contacting us.
  • Limit sensitive information use: Health-related data is sensitive personal information under CPRA. We use it only to provide the Services and as described in this policy.
  • Non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Send requests to support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”) or the Privacy Officer. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days. You may designate an authorized agent. We may require proof of authorization and may still verify your identity directly.

Washington residents: My Health My Data Act

The Washington My Health My Data Act provides additional protections for consumer health data:

  • Consent: Where the Washington My Health My Data Act applies, we collect and share consumer health data only with your consent or as necessary to provide the Services you request.
  • Access: You may request a copy of consumer health data collected about you.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of consumer health data.
  • Withdrawal: You may withdraw consent to collection or sharing at any time.
  • No geofencing: We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities to collect consumer health data or deliver advertising.

Send requests to support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”).

Nevada residents

Nevada residents may ask us not to sell the covered information they give us. FlatRx does not sell personal information.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”).

Additional state privacy rights

Requests under the following state laws may be sent to support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”):

  • Colorado Privacy Act: Access, correct, delete, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and profiling. You may appeal a denial by contacting us.
  • Connecticut Data Privacy Act: Access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling.
  • Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act: Access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling. You may appeal a denial and, if unsatisfied, contact the Virginia Attorney General.
  • Oregon Consumer Privacy Act: Access, correct, delete, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling, including a list of third parties receiving your data.
  • Texas Data Privacy and Security Act: Access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling.

If your state has consumer privacy legislation and is not listed, contact us about your rights.

Do not sell or share my personal information

FlatRx does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To submit a request concerning sale or sharing of personal information, email support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”) with the subject “Do Not Share.”

Your choices

You may contact the Privacy Officer to exercise the rights described in this policy. Browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies; blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality.

If we send you marketing emails, each one includes a link to unsubscribe, and you can also opt out by emailing support@flatrx.io.

Data retention

Personal information is retained as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements. Health records are retained according to applicable federal and state medical record retention laws. Account information is retained for the account's duration and a reasonable period afterward to meet legal and business obligations. Your provider group and the dispensing pharmacy keep medical and pharmacy records for the periods their licensing laws require.

Data security

Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards are used to protect personal information. No Internet transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The Services are not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn that information from a person under 18 has been collected, we will close the account and delete that information promptly, except records that the law requires the provider, pharmacy or us to keep.
