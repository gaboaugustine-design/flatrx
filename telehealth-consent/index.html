Consent to Telehealth

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Your provider and FlatRx

This consent concerns healthcare services delivered through telehealth by independent licensed providers. Provider group: Wasef Health, PC.

FlatRx facilitates access to providers through its storefront and the FlatRx patient portal. FlatRx does not practice medicine, diagnose, give clinical advice, prescribe, compound, or dispense medication. Your provider makes all clinical decisions using independent professional judgment.

What telehealth involves

Telehealth uses electronic communications to deliver healthcare services when you and your provider are in different physical locations. It may involve:

  • Electronic transmission and review of medical records, images, and health information.
  • Audio, video, or data communications.
  • Information from medical devices.

These are forms of telehealth; the form appropriate for your care depends on your provider's judgment and the Services available. You must provide complete, accurate, and current information about your health and medications and respond to your provider's questions.

Potential benefits

Telehealth can allow you to communicate with a licensed provider and share health information without being in the same location. Your provider can review the information you submit and discuss whether treatment is appropriate. These are potential benefits of the method of communication, not guarantees of treatment or outcomes.

Risks and limitations

  • Your provider may be unable to perform an examination or obtain information that would be available in person. In-person evaluation or other care may be needed.
  • Problems with Internet access, devices, software, or electronic communications may interrupt or delay a visit or the receipt of information.
  • Electronic transmission of health information carries privacy and security risks. Security protocols help protect information, but no Internet transmission is completely secure.
  • Telehealth may not be appropriate for your condition. Your provider may decide that treatment through telehealth is not appropriate.

No guarantee of a prescription

An online visit does not guarantee eligibility, a prescription, or a particular result. An independent licensed provider may prescribe compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide only if the provider finds it appropriate after the visit.

US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacies prepare and ship prescribed medication; the pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. They are prepared by state-licensed pharmacies based on a valid prescription from a licensed provider. Compounded medications are not therapeutically equivalent to FDA-approved products. Review the information your provider gives you and ask about risks, contraindications, and side effects before use.

Emergencies

Telehealth through FlatRx is not for urgent or emergency care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately. Do not wait for a portal response.

Records and privacy

Your provider documents clinical decisions and information received during your care. Our Privacy Policy describes the use and disclosure of personal and protected health information, record access, confidential communications, and other patient rights. Wasef Health, PC is responsible for your medical records; FlatRx and our technology platform provider handle your information on its behalf under business associate agreements.

Protect the confidentiality of your account credentials and use a private setting for telehealth communications. You may ask questions about how your information is handled by messaging the care team in the FlatRx patient portal or emailing support@flatrx.io (subject line “Privacy Request”).

Questions, alternatives, and withdrawal

You may ask your provider questions about telehealth, its risks and limitations, and whether in-person care is appropriate. Telehealth is not a substitute for in-person care in all cases.

You may decline telehealth or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent does not by itself cancel your plan or stop renewals; to stop billing, cancel as described in our Cancellation Policy. Notify your provider through the FlatRx patient portal; for help contacting your provider, email support at support@flatrx.io.

For plan billing, cancellation, and refund terms, review our Terms of Service, Cancellation Policy, and Refund Policy.

Your acknowledgement

By giving this consent, you acknowledge that you have reviewed the nature, potential benefits, risks, and limitations of telehealth; have had the opportunity to ask your provider questions; understand that a prescription and results are not guaranteed; and agree to receive care through telehealth from your independent licensed provider. You understand that you may withdraw consent as described above.

You give this consent in your online visit, before your information is sent to a provider, and a record of it is kept with your account.
