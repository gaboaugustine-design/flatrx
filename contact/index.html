Medical questions

Clinical questions go to your provider. Message your provider through the FlatRx patient portal. FlatRx staff don't give medical advice.

If you are having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Don't wait for a reply in the portal or by email.

Who provides your care

Medical care is provided by independent licensed providers of Wasef Health, PC, an independent medical group. Your provider decides whether treatment is appropriate and, if so, what to prescribe. FlatRx does not practice medicine, prescribe, compound or dispense medication.

Who prepares your medication

If your provider prescribes, a US-licensed 503A compounding pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to you. The pharmacy that dispenses your order is named on your pharmacy label.

Where FlatRx is available

FlatRx is available in every US state except California.
